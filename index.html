The damaged quote funnel

This is one of your live “Get a quote” links

194 of these links carry raw spaces inside the web address. A space is an illegal character in a URL — browsers quietly forgive it, search engines do not.

https://pureologycleaner.co.uk/contact-us.php?service=Cleaning␣in␣Leeds

Why it matters: these links are how a visitor moves from a service page to your quote form. When the busiest conversion path on the site is flagged as broken 194 times, it undermines how search engines score every page that carries it.
